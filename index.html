How the glove works

The movement starts with the patient's own effort.

  1. The patient tries to moveEMG sensors pick up the muscle signal.
  2. The controller reads the signalIt works out which movement was intended.
  3. Linear motors respondThey pull and release inside a soft, flexible glove.
  4. Fingers flex and extendTherapy that follows the patient's own effort.
